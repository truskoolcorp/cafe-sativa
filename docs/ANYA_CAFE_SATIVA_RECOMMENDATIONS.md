# Café Sativa — Anya/OC editorial recommendation governance

Owner/founder: **Keith**. All decisions that require owner approval remain held until explicitly approved.

- **Anya / OpenClaw (OC)** is the internal analyst and orchestrator. Recommendations must be traceable to observed evidence, disclose uncertainty, and be ranked for audience value, editorial fit, costs, rights and canonical readiness. No empty-evidence auto-suggestions.
- **Laviche** is the lead venue hostess and concierge. She presents approved experiences and welcomes guests; she is not displaced by the analytical agent.
- **Specialist hosts** own their established canonical niches and may contribute as supporting hosts to related experiences. Assignments are provisional until checked against live GLYPH character canon. Do not override likeness, voices, relationships or identity.
- **Keith** retains brand direction and all mandatory owner decisions.
- Suggested content is **not** a scheduled event or permission to render, purchase, syndicate, or publish. A recommendation moves through evidence review, canonical verification, proposal approval, production QA and publication authorization.
- Recommendation display of *already approved* library items is separate from proposing *new* content. Members only see published, approved assets for their entitlement; no raw personal behavioral data leaves the authorized analytics boundary.

Engineering reference: `lib/content/recommendation-roles.ts`. This policy file does not by itself implement an automated crawler, engagement analysis, scheduling or publishing agent.
