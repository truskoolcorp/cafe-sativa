# Gallery collection release requirements

All collections are proposals until rights, economics, inventory, and delivery are approved. No live mint or payment activation is authorized by this document.

| Collection | Product | Release gate |
|---|---|---|
| Founders | Limited collectible with defined Gallery perks | Approved edition size, price, benefit duration and fulfillment |
| Resident artists | Artist-authorized digital editions | Signed rights and 90/10 eligible sale allocation |
| Physical companion | Physical art plus optional token | Shipping, returns and token redemption policy |
| Exhibition series | Exhibition-specific digital collectible | Confirmed exhibition and rights |
| Cultural collaborations | Dallas–Tenerife collaborative editions | Partner agreements and rights |

## Customer experience
- Traditional art browsing and checkout remain independent of wallets.
- Clearly distinguish digital certificates from actual on-chain tokens.
- Show chain, contract, edition, rights, delivery status and fees before purchase.
- No investment-return promises or guaranteed secondary royalties.
- Zaria must answer from approved catalog and benefit records, never invent availability or provenance.

## Acceptance gates
- Confirm artist approval, Stripe Connect readiness, stock reservation, payment webhook idempotency, refunds and delivery.
- Verify wallet signatures, nonce replay protection, approved contract ownership and revocation after transfer.
- Test mobile, keyboard access, reduced motion, unavailable wallet and RPC outages.
- Review contract security and costs before deployment; no production merge or spending without separate approval.
